# Reviewer 1 C35-C37: run-level inference

Independent experimental units are fresh seeded training/evaluation
replicates, not ticks, episodes, packets or multiple scenarios using
one trained checkpoint. Match algorithms within each scenario and
replicate block. Seed labels do not prove shared random realizations,
exchangeability or independent runs: randomize/counterbalance order,
restart processes and Mininet, record failures and runtime conditions.
For training curves use independent initializations; repeated evaluation
scenarios for a seed are correlated, not extra independent replicates.

Proposed main benchmark n=10 independent seeds, subject to a locked
budget/precision decision using calibration, never significance-driven
extension on the held-out set. Five-seed exploratory studies remain
acceptable if uncertainty and limits are explicit. With five nonzero
paired differences the smallest two-sided exact sign-flip p is .0625,
so a 5% rejection is impossible even before multiplicity correction.
Ten seeds do not guarantee adequate power. Predeclare practically
relevant differences, pilot variance and precision/budget if feasible.

Summaries retain individual seed values, n/expected/missing, mean,
sample SD (n-1), median, quartiles, range and a two-sided 95% Student-t
interval for the mean. The t interval assumes independent approximately
normal units/differences; with small n/skew it is approximate and not
robust. Zero sample variance is flagged, not proof of zero uncertainty.
Bounds are not clipped to physical ranges. Pointwise episode intervals
are not simultaneous bands or a convergence test.

Primary inferential contrasts: candidate DQN minus baseline run-level
loss (percentage points) and successful-echo RTT (ms), lower is better.
Use two-sided sign-flip test of the mean difference, exact up to 16
nonzero pairs, reproducible Monte Carlo above with +1 correction.
The null requires independent sign-symmetric paired differences or
genuine within-block exchangeability; matching seed IDs alone is
insufficient. Not a distribution-free test of equal means. Report
effect and CI, all points, and assumptions; no mechanical claims.
Holm correction across the predeclared primary family. Unadjusted
95% intervals are not simultaneous. Secondary windows/metrics and
sensitivity/scalability reports are exploratory unless explicitly
predeclared. Never test all episodes. No p-value on incomplete pairs.

Relative reduction = 100*(mean baseline - mean candidate)/mean baseline,
using the same complete matched seed set, scenario and inclusive
episode window. Not mean of individual ratios. Undefined if baseline
mean is zero or any missing pair. Absolute difference and 95% interval
are the main effect estimate. Do not compute relative reductions for
signed reward. Negative reduction denotes deterioration.

Probe-on/off equivalence is a different question: prespecify a positive
practical margin for each metric, use paired 90% t CI inside both
margins (TOST logic) with independent approximately normal differences.
Not rejecting a difference is not equivalence. The helper is exploratory
until margins, endpoints and any multiplicity control are locked.
No equivalence claim from incomplete or constant-difference samples.
